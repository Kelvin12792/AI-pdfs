## The Problem This Product Solves

Millions of people globally — students, small business owners, non-tech employees, job seekers, and educators — are aware that AI is changing the world around them but have no accessible entry point into understanding it. The internet is flooded with AI content that is either:

- Too technical (written for developers and researchers)
- Too shallow (listicles and hype with no depth)
- Too theoretical (concepts with no practical applications)
- Fragmented (no structured learning path from zero to confident)

The result is that the majority of the global population feels locked out of AI literacy. They believe the misconception: **"AI is too complicated to understand."**

This product exists to shatter that belief.

---

## The Solution

A structured, open-ended series of 20+ self-directed PDF learning documents that take a complete beginner — someone with zero technical background — from "I don't understand AI at all" to "I can understand, use, and explain AI confidently."

Every PDF in the series:
- Opens with a real-life analogy that creates an immediate aha moment
- Teaches one focused AI concept or application per edition
- Uses plain language, diagrams, real-world examples, and step-by-step breakdowns
- Ends with a quiz, a reflection question, a glossary, and a teaser for the next edition
- Is written so that it can be directly converted into a 2–5 minute viral explainer video

## Constraints

- **No technical jargon without explanation.** Every technical term must be defined in the glossary and explained in plain language at first use.
- **No plagiarism.** All Twitter source content must be fully paraphrased and rewritten in original voice. No direct reproduction of tweets.
- **No unverified statistics.** All factual claims must be fact-checked before publication. Uncertain claims must be flagged and either verified or removed.
- **No deviation from design system.** All PDFs must follow DESIGN_GUIDELINES.md and STYLE_TOKENS.yaml exactly.
- **No skipping the pilot.** PDF 01 must be completed, reviewed, and approved before any subsequent editions are produced.
- **Bi-weekly release cadence.** One new PDF every two weeks once production begins.
- **Portrait A4 format only.** No landscape, no narrow mobile format — Portrait A4 optimised for all reading devices.
- **English only** until translations are commissioned.
- **Anthropic brand color palette** — no deviation.
