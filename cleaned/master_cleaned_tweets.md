### Post 11 | A 30-Day Roadmap for Learning AI Fundamentals
- **Tier:** Spans ALL tiers (Foundational → Advanced)
- **Editions:** 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, 18
- **Key concepts:**
  - **Tokenization:** AI breaks text into small pieces called tokens — like cutting a sentence into puzzle pieces
  - **Attention mechanism:** AI weighs word relationships — "sat" connects to "cat" and "mat"
  - **Context windows:** AI's working memory has a limit — like only remembering the last 50 pages of a book
  - **Temperature:** A creativity dial — low = precise and predictable, high = creative and surprising
  - **Hallucination:** AI predicts likely text, not verified truth — it can confidently fabricate information
  - **Model landscape:** Different AI models for different tasks — like tools in a toolbox
  - **Prompt engineering:** How you phrase requests changes everything
  - **AI image/video generation:** Describe what you want, AI creates it
  - **AI-assisted coding:** Describe in English, AI writes code
  - **Open-source/local AI:** Run models on your own computer for privacy
- **Best analogies:** Context window as short-term memory; temperature as musician (sheet music vs. jazz); tokenization as puzzle pieces; model landscape as toolbox
- **Takeaway for series:** The single richest source in Batch 1. Contributes to 16+ editions.
