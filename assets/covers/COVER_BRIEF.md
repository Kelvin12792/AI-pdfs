# Cover Design Brief: The AI Basics Nobody Made Clear

## Final Cover Text Elements

| Element | Text | Font | Size | Color |
|---------|------|------|------|-------|
| Title | The AI Basics Nobody Made Clear | Inter Bold | 36pt | #CC785C (coral) |
| Subtitle | A Beginner's Guide to AI | Inter Regular | 14pt | #FFFFFF (white) |
| Author | by Kelvin M, AI Educator & Researcher | Inter Medium | 12pt | #888888 (muted) |

## Cover Layout

- Top 60%: Full-bleed AI-generated abstract illustration
- Bottom 40%: Dark band (#1A1A1A) containing all text elements
- No text in the illustration itself
- Text is overlaid on the dark band only

## Cover Image Requirements

- Abstract conceptual illustration
- Coral (#CC785C) and cream (#F2D4C8) colour palette
- Flat, minimal design aesthetic
- No people, no text, no typography in the image
- Must communicate "AI made accessible" or "clarity from complexity"
- Must work at small sizes (WhatsApp thumbnail, social media preview)
- Final dimensions: 2480 x 3508px, 300 DPI, PNG, RGB
