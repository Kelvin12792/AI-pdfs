### 4. Body Sections

#### Section 1: What is AI? (The One-Sentence Answer)
- **Core definition:** "AI is software that learns from examples instead of following fixed instructions."
- **Analogy:** Child learning to tell cats from dogs — no written rules, just examples
- **Comparison:** Calculator (fixed rules) vs AI (learns patterns)
- **Diagram 1:** Two-column flowchart — "Regular Software" (input → fixed rules → output) vs "AI" (examples → learns patterns → handles new situations)
- **Callout (DID YOU KNOW?):** Term "artificial intelligence" coined in 1956 at Dartmouth College
- **Source posts:** Post 27 (AI as multiplier), Post 10 (you don't need to keep up with every update)

#### Section 2: AI is Already in Your Life
- **Format:** Table of 6 real-world AI examples (phone keyboard, email spam filter, Netflix/Spotify, maps, social media, online shopping)
- **Emotional hook:** Most people have never intentionally used AI — you're early, not late
- **Callout (KEY FACT):** AI is a collection of techniques, not one single technology
- **Source posts:** Post 52 ("you're not late, you're early"), Post 12 (learning by doing)

#### Section 3: How is AI Different from Regular Software?
- **Format:** Comparison table (5 rows) — how it learns, handling new situations, improving over time, example, best for
- **Analogy:** Basic thermostat (fixed rule) vs smart assistant (learns and adapts)
- **Key distinction:** Regular software follows human-written rules; AI creates its own rules from examples
- **Callout (IN THE REAL WORLD):** Email spam filters — 20+ years old, catches ~99% of spam
- **Source posts:** Post 6 (clear input = better output)

#### Section 4: Why is Everyone Talking About AI Now?
- **Framework:** Three convergences that happened simultaneously
  1. Massive data — internet/social media/smartphones created unprecedented volumes
  2. Powerful computers — GPU hardware breakthrough made training practical
  3. Breakthrough methods — deep learning unlocked language, images, generation
- **Key insight:** Feels sudden, but the foundation was 70 years in the making
- **Diagram 2:** Three converging arrows → central circle ("AI Becomes Accessible"), with timeline 1956 → decades of research → 2020s convergence
- **Callout (WATCH OUT):** AI is an evolution, not a revolution — understanding this prevents hype-driven panic
- **Source posts:** Post 16 (architecture-first thinking)

#### Section 5: Try It Yourself — Your First AI Conversation
- **Format:** 7-step practical walkthrough
  1. Open browser
  2. Go to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini (free)
  3. Create free account (~60 seconds)
  4. Type: "Explain artificial intelligence to me like I am 10 years old"
  5. Read the response — notice the simple language
  6. Follow up: "Give me three examples of AI I probably used today"
  7. Notice context retention — AI remembers the conversation
- **Key insight:** Difference between Google search (links) and AI (answers) = encyclopedia vs knowledgeable friend
- **Callout (DID YOU KNOW?):** Search engine vs AI conversation comparison
- **Source posts:** Post 24 (54-year-old consultant success story), Post 1 (tiered learning), Post 2 (free resources)
