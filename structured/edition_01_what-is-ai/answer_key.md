## Quiz Answers

**1.** B — Software that learns from examples instead of following fixed instructions

**2.** C — Netflix recommending a show based on what you watched before

**3.** B — Massive data, powerful computers, breakthrough methods

**4.** False — AI research began in the 1950s. The term was coined in 1956 at Dartmouth College. What is new is that AI became powerful and accessible enough for ordinary people to use.

**5.** False — Modern AI tools are designed for anyone to use. You interact with them by typing in plain language, not by writing code.

**6.** examples (also acceptable: data, examples and data)

**8.** Sample answer: Regular software follows fixed rules that a programmer wrote — it can only do exactly what it was told. AI software learns patterns from data and can handle new situations it was never specifically programmed for.

**9.** Sample answer: My phone's keyboard predicts what I am going to type next. Before reading this edition, I did not think of that as AI — I thought it was just a built-in feature of the phone.

**10.** Sample answer: Three things came together at the same time — the internet created massive amounts of data, computers became powerful enough to process it, and researchers developed breakthrough methods like deep learning. The foundation was decades in the making, but the pieces only aligned recently.
