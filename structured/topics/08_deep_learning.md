### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Deep learning = neural networks with many layers.** The more layers, the more complex patterns the network can learn. Modern AI models have billions of parameters (adjustable weights) spread across hundreds of layers.
- **Three breakthroughs that made deep learning work:** (1) Massive data — the internet created more training data than anyone imagined, (2) GPU computing — graphics cards turned out to be perfect for the math deep learning needs, (3) Better algorithms — researchers figured out how to train very deep networks without them collapsing.
- **Types of deep learning architectures:** Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) — excel at images and visual patterns. Recurrent neural networks (RNNs) — handle sequences like text and speech. Transformers — the architecture behind ChatGPT, Claude, and most modern AI. Transformers process all words simultaneously rather than one at a time.
- **The transformer revolution (2017):** Google researchers published "Attention Is All You Need" — a paper that introduced the transformer architecture. This single paper is arguably the most important AI publication of the decade. It made modern chatbots, image generators, and code assistants possible.
