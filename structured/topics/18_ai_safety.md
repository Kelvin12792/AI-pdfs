### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Hallucination is a safety issue:** AI confidently producing false information is not just annoying — it is dangerous in healthcare, legal, and financial contexts. Users must learn to verify.
- **Security risks:** AI can be tricked (prompt injection), used to generate malware, or exploited to extract private data. As AI becomes more powerful, security becomes more critical.
- **Alignment problem:** How do we ensure AI does what we actually want, not just what we literally asked? The gap between instructions and intentions is one of the hardest problems in AI.
- **Concentration of power:** A few companies control the most powerful AI. This concentration raises questions about monopoly, access, and democratic control of technology.
- **The balanced view:** AI is a tool. Like any tool, it can be used well or poorly. The goal is not to fear AI but to understand it well enough to use it safely and push for responsible development.
