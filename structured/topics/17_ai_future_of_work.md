### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Jobs AI will likely automate:** Routine data entry, basic customer service, simple content generation, appointment scheduling, basic bookkeeping, some manufacturing tasks. The pattern: repetitive, predictable, data-based tasks.
- **Jobs AI will likely create:** AI trainers, prompt engineers, AI ethics officers, human-AI interaction designers, AI audit specialists, synthetic data managers. The pattern: roles that require human judgment about AI systems.
- **Jobs AI will likely transform (not replace):** Doctors (AI assists diagnosis), lawyers (AI assists research), teachers (AI personalizes learning), marketers (AI generates content), developers (AI writes code). The pattern: human expertise + AI tools = enhanced performance.
- **The real message:** AI does not replace people. It replaces tasks. People who learn to work with AI will replace people who refuse to.
