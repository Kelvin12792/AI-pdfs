### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **What AI actually is:** AI is software that can learn from data and make decisions or predictions without being explicitly programmed for every scenario. At its core, AI looks at large amounts of examples, finds patterns, and uses those patterns to handle new situations.
- **The three types of AI people encounter daily:** Recommendation systems (Netflix, Spotify), virtual assistants (Siri, Alexa), and autocomplete/predictive text. Most people have already used AI without realizing it.
- **Brief history hook:** The term "artificial intelligence" was coined in 1956 at Dartmouth College. AI is not new — what is new is that it is now powerful enough and accessible enough for ordinary people to use.
- **Why now:** Three things converged — massive amounts of data, powerful computers, and breakthrough algorithms. This is why AI seems to have appeared "suddenly" even though researchers have been working on it for 70 years.
