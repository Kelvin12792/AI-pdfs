### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **AI in medical diagnosis:** AI can analyze medical images (X-rays, MRIs, CT scans) and detect patterns that human doctors sometimes miss. In some studies, AI matches or exceeds specialist accuracy for specific conditions like skin cancer detection and diabetic eye disease.
- **Drug discovery:** Traditional drug development takes 10-15 years. AI is helping researchers identify promising drug candidates in months by simulating molecular interactions.
- **AI health assistants:** Chatbots that help patients describe symptoms, schedule appointments, and manage chronic conditions. Not replacing doctors — providing 24/7 support between visits.
- **Mental health:** AI-powered therapy companions and mood-tracking apps are making mental health support more accessible. They are not substitutes for professional therapy but can fill gaps in access.
- **Critical caution:** AI in healthcare is the highest-stakes application. Errors can be life-threatening. All AI health tools must be validated by medical professionals. This edition must emphasize that AI assists doctors — it does not replace them.
