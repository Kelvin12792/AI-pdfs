### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **The AI productivity stack (2026):** Writing (ChatGPT, Claude), Research (Perplexity, Google AI), Spreadsheets (Claude for Excel, Sheets AI), Presentations (Gamma, Beautiful.ai), Image creation (Midjourney, DALL-E), Meeting notes (Otter, Fireflies), Email (Gmail AI, Outlook Copilot).
- **The 80/20 of AI tools:** Most people only need 2-3 AI tools to transform their productivity. The key is depth over breadth — learn one tool well before adding another.
- **The iteration mindset:** The most productive AI users do not accept the first answer. They treat AI like a collaborator: generate → review → refine → repeat. Three rounds of revision typically produces excellent results.
