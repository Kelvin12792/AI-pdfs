### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **AI in small business (2026 reality):** Customer service chatbots, automated email marketing, AI-powered bookkeeping, inventory prediction, social media content scheduling. These tools cost a fraction of hiring staff.
- **The "one-person company" model:** AI makes it possible for a single person to operate what used to require a team of 5-10. Content creation, customer support, data analysis, and scheduling can all be partially or fully automated.
- **Revenue disclaimer:** Revenue claims from social media are self-reported and often unverifiable. The concepts are valid; specific dollar figures should be treated as illustrative, not guaranteed.
