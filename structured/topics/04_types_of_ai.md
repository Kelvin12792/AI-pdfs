### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Narrow AI (ANI):** All AI that exists today. Designed to do one thing well — play chess, recommend movies, translate languages. It cannot do anything outside its specific training.
- **General AI (AGI):** A theoretical AI that could do any intellectual task a human can do. Does not exist yet. This is what people imagine when they think of "AI" from science fiction.
- **Superintelligent AI (ASI):** A theoretical AI smarter than all humans combined. Purely hypothetical. Important for the ethics discussion in later editions.
- **Practical categories people encounter:** Generative AI (creates new content — text, images, video), predictive AI (forecasts outcomes — weather, stock trends), conversational AI (chatbots and assistants), computer vision (understands images and video), robotics AI (controls physical machines).
- **The chatbot-to-agent spectrum:** AI is moving from tools you ask (chatbots) to tools that act (agents). This is one of the most important shifts happening right now.
