### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Verify before you share:** Treat AI output like a first draft from an intern — check facts, verify sources, review logic. Never publish AI output without human review.
- **Disclose AI use:** Be transparent when AI helped create something — in work, school, or public content. Honesty builds trust.
- **Protect your data:** Be cautious about what personal information you share with AI. Read privacy policies. Use local AI for sensitive work when possible.
- **Fight bias actively:** Be aware that AI can reinforce biases. Question outputs that feel stereotypical or unfair. Report biased AI behavior.
- **Stay human:** Use AI to free up time for things that matter — relationships, creativity, rest. Do not let AI replace the parts of life that make you human.
- **The reader's AI code of conduct:** A practical checklist readers can adopt for their own AI use.
