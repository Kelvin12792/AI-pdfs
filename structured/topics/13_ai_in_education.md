### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **AI as personal tutor:** AI can explain concepts at any level, answer follow-up questions endlessly, and adapt explanations to the learner's pace. Unlike a classroom, AI never moves on until you are ready.
- **AI in classrooms (2026):** Automated grading, personalized learning paths, real-time translation for multilingual classrooms, AI teaching assistants that handle routine questions so teachers focus on complex ones.
- **The plagiarism question:** AI can write essays, solve math problems, and complete assignments. Schools are still figuring out how to handle this. The consensus is shifting from "ban AI" to "teach students to use AI responsibly."
- **Lifelong learning:** AI makes it possible to learn any subject, at any age, for free. The traditional "go to school, get a degree, stop learning" model is being replaced by continuous, AI-assisted learning.
