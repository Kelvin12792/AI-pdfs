### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **AGI timeline:** Expert predictions for artificial general intelligence range from 5 to 50+ years. No consensus exists. What matters for readers: current narrow AI is already transformative.
- **Multimodal AI:** AI that processes text, images, audio, and video simultaneously. Already here in 2026, getting better rapidly. The future AI will understand the world through multiple senses, like humans do.
- **AI agents as default:** The shift from chatbots to agents will continue. Within 5 years, most people will have AI agents managing parts of their digital life — scheduling, shopping, monitoring, communicating.
- **Personalized AI:** AI that knows you — your preferences, communication style, goals. Personal AI assistants that adapt to you over time rather than being generic.
- **AI regulation:** Governments worldwide are racing to regulate AI. The EU AI Act, US executive orders, and China's AI regulations will shape what AI can and cannot do in different countries.
- **The democratization trend:** AI tools are getting cheaper, more accessible, and easier to use. The barrier to entry drops every year. This means more people creating with AI, more innovation, and more competition.
