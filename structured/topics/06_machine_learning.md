### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Three types of machine learning:** Supervised (learns from labeled examples — like flashcards with answers), unsupervised (finds patterns in unlabeled data — like sorting a pile of mixed coins), reinforcement (learns by trial and error with rewards — like training a dog with treats).
- **The training process simplified:** (1) Feed the AI thousands of examples, (2) AI makes predictions, (3) AI checks how wrong it was, (4) AI adjusts itself to be less wrong, (5) Repeat millions of times. The result: an AI that can make accurate predictions on data it has never seen before.
- **Overfitting:** When AI memorizes the training data instead of learning the patterns. Like a student who memorizes answers to specific exam questions but cannot answer a rephrased version of the same question.
- **Why data quality matters:** Garbage in, garbage out. If AI trains on biased or low-quality data, it produces biased or low-quality results. This is one of the most important concepts in all of AI.
