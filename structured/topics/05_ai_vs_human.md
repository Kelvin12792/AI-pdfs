### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **Speed vs. understanding:** AI can process millions of data points per second, but it does not "understand" any of them the way a human does. A child who has seen one dog can recognize all dogs. AI needs thousands of examples.
- **Creativity:** AI generates new combinations of existing patterns. Humans create from emotion, experience, and intuition. AI can write a poem in the style of Shakespeare — but it has never felt heartbreak.
- **Common sense:** Humans effortlessly know that "time flies like an arrow" is a metaphor, while AI may struggle with this. Common sense reasoning remains one of AI's biggest weaknesses.
- **Physical world understanding:** AI can describe a glass of water but does not know what "wet" feels like. It has no sensory experience.
- **Ethics and moral judgment:** AI has no sense of right or wrong. It follows patterns in data, which may contain biases. Humans must remain the ethical decision-makers.
