### Research-Based Additions (Claude Knowledge)

- **AI you already use without knowing it:** Netflix recommendations, Spotify Discover Weekly, Google Maps traffic predictions, phone autocorrect, email spam filtering, social media feeds, fraud detection on your credit card.
- **Smart home AI:** Voice assistants (Alexa, Google Home, Siri), smart thermostats that learn your schedule, security cameras with facial recognition.
- **AI in your phone:** Portrait mode (AI separates foreground from background), night mode photography (AI enhances low-light images), voice-to-text, real-time translation.
- **The invisible layer:** Most AI in daily life is invisible — you benefit from it without interacting with it directly. This is by design. The best AI fades into the background.
